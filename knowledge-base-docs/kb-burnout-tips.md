# Burnout Prevention & Recovery: Evidence-Based Tips

## Understanding Burnout

Burnout is a state of emotional, physical, and mental exhaustion caused by prolonged stress. The World Health Organization (WHO) recognizes it as an occupational phenomenon characterized by three dimensions:

1. **Exhaustion** — Feeling drained and unable to cope
2. **Cynicism** — Detachment and negative feelings about work
3. **Reduced efficacy** — Decreased productivity and confidence

---

## The Science of Breaks

### Why Breaks Matter

Your brain can only maintain focused attention for 90–120 minutes before performance degrades. This is called the **Ultradian Rhythm**. Without breaks:

- Cortisol levels remain elevated
- Decision fatigue accumulates
- Creative problem-solving declines by up to 50%
- Error rates increase significantly

### Optimal Break Patterns

| Technique | Work Period | Break Duration | Best For |
|-----------|------------|----------------|----------|
| Pomodoro | 25 min | 5 min | Deep focus tasks |
| 52-17 | 52 min | 17 min | Creative work |
| 90-minute blocks | 90 min | 20 min | Complex problem solving |
| 50-10 | 50 min | 10 min | General productivity |

### What Makes a Break Effective

**Do:**
- Step away from your screen
- Move your body (even a 2-minute walk helps)
- Look at distant objects (reduces eye strain)
- Practice deep breathing (4-7-8 technique)
- Hydrate and have a healthy snack

**Don't:**
- Scroll social media (doesn't rest the brain)
- Check work email (maintains stress response)
- Sit in the same position
- Drink excessive caffeine

## Screen Time & Eye Health

### The 20-20-20 Rule

Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. This reduces digital eye strain by allowing your ciliary muscles to relax.

### Blue Light Management

- Enable night mode/blue light filter after 6 PM
- Use the minimum brightness comfortable for your environment
- Consider anti-glare screen protectors
- Maintain arm's length distance from screens

### Optimal Screen Settings

- **Brightness:** Match your surrounding environment
- **Text size:** Large enough to read without squinting
- **Contrast:** High contrast for readability
- **Color temperature:** Warm tones in the evening

## Physical Wellness for Desk Workers

### Posture Checklist

- [ ] Feet flat on the floor
- [ ] Knees at 90-degree angle
- [ ] Screen at eye level
- [ ] Shoulders relaxed, not hunched
- [ ] Wrists neutral while typing
- [ ] Back supported by chair

### Micro-Exercises (Do at Your Desk)

1. **Neck rolls** — 5 slow circles each direction
2. **Shoulder shrugs** — Raise, hold 5 seconds, release. Repeat 10x
3. **Wrist circles** — 10 each direction
4. **Seated spinal twist** — Hold 15 seconds each side
5. **Calf raises** — 15 reps while seated or standing
6. **Eye palming** — Rub hands together, cup over eyes for 30 seconds

### Movement Goals

- Stand up every 30 minutes
- Walk for 5 minutes every 2 hours
- Do 10 minutes of stretching in the morning
- Aim for 7,000–10,000 steps daily

## Mental Health Strategies

### Stress Management Techniques

**Box Breathing (Navy SEALs technique):**
1. Inhale for 4 seconds
2. Hold for 4 seconds
3. Exhale for 4 seconds
4. Hold for 4 seconds
5. Repeat 4–6 cycles

**Progressive Muscle Relaxation:**
1. Tense each muscle group for 5 seconds
2. Release and notice the contrast
3. Start from toes, work up to head
4. Takes about 10 minutes

### Cognitive Reframing

Replace negative thought patterns:

| Instead of... | Try... |
|---------------|--------|
| "I have to do this" | "I get to do this" |
| "This is impossible" | "This is challenging, let me break it down" |
| "I'm failing" | "I'm learning and improving" |
| "I must be perfect" | "Progress matters more than perfection" |
| "I don't have time" | "This isn't my priority right now" |

### Digital Detox Recommendations

- No screens 1 hour before bed
- Designate phone-free meals
- Turn off non-essential notifications
- Use app timers for social media (max 30 min/day)
- Weekend digital sabbath (4–8 hours screen-free)

## Sleep Optimization

### Sleep Hygiene Checklist

- [ ] Consistent bedtime (even on weekends)
- [ ] Cool room temperature (65–68°F / 18–20°C)
- [ ] Complete darkness
- [ ] No caffeine after 2 PM
- [ ] No heavy meals 3 hours before bed
- [ ] Limit alcohol (disrupts REM sleep)
- [ ] No screens 30–60 minutes before bed

### Sleep Debt Recovery

- You need 2–3 days of normal sleep per hour of sleep debt
- You cannot fully "catch up" on weekends
- Consistent sleep schedule beats total hours

## Nutrition for Energy & Focus

### Brain-Boosting Foods

- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — Salmon, walnuts, flaxseed
- **Antioxidants** — Blueberries, dark chocolate, green tea
- **Complex carbohydrates** — Oats, quinoa, sweet potatoes
- **Protein** — Eggs, lean meats, legumes
- **Magnesium** — Spinach, almonds, avocado

### Hydration

- Drink 8 glasses (2L) of water daily
- Dehydration reduces cognitive performance by up to 25%
- Keep a water bottle at your desk
- herbal tea counts toward hydration

### Energy-Boosting Snacks

- Apple slices with almond butter
- Trail mix (nuts, seeds, dark chocolate)
- Greek yogurt with berries
- Hummus with vegetable sticks
- Dark chocolate (70%+ cocoa)

## Building Resilience

### Daily Habits for Long-Term Wellness

1. **Morning routine** — 15 minutes of intentional starting
2. **Gratitude journaling** — Write 3 things you're grateful for
3. **Physical movement** — At least 30 minutes daily
4. **Social connection** — Meaningful conversation with someone
5. **Learning** — 20 minutes of skill development
6. **Reflection** — 5 minutes reviewing the day

### Setting Boundaries

- Learn to say no without guilt
- Define clear work hours
- Separate work and personal spaces
- Communicate your limits clearly
- Protect your recovery time

### Warning Signs of Burnout

Seek help if you experience:
- Chronic exhaustion that doesn't improve with rest
- Detachment from work you used to enjoy
- Declining performance despite increased effort
- Physical symptoms (headaches, stomach issues, insomnia)
- Increased irritability or emotional volatility
- Loss of motivation and sense of purpose

---

## When to Seek Professional Help

Consider talking to a mental health professional if:
- Burnout symptoms persist for more than 2 weeks
- You experience anxiety or depression
- Sleep problems continue despite good hygiene
- You use alcohol or substances to cope
- You feel hopeless about your situation
- Physical symptoms don't have medical explanation

**Resources:**
- Employee Assistance Programs (EAP)
- Mental health hotlines
- Licensed therapists specializing in occupational stress
- Support groups for professionals

## Quick Reference Card

### The 5-Minute Reset

When you feel overwhelmed right now:
1. Take 3 deep breaths (4-7-8 pattern)
2. Drink a glass of water
3. Stand up and stretch
4. Look out a window for 30 seconds
5. Write down your top 3 priorities

### The Weekly Reset

Every Sunday:
1. Review the past week — what worked, what didn't
2. Plan the coming week with realistic goals
3. Schedule breaks and self-care activities
4. Prepare healthy meals/snacks in advance
5. Connect with someone you care about

---

*Remember: Productivity without wellness is just a countdown to burnout. Take care of yourself first — everything else follows.*
